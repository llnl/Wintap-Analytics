## Behavioral Notes
- Initial 13/16 and 18/19 results exposed a Pester filtered-mock leak and incorrect logman help-exit assumption; fixtures corrected.
- Capture/waits are mocked; actual checks cover provider metadata, invalid-command diagnostics and non-elevated refusal only, with no ETW session/workload or proof of PowerShell 7 installation/live emission.
- Default 60 seconds, range 1-120, sequential ETL cap 64 MB; provider GUIDs need no manifest registration, but live capture/loss remains unverified.
- Ownership precedes logging; original/cleanup errors survive, failed post-stop queries do not prove absence, and forced kill/crash needs manual recovery.txt handling.
- Suffixed ETLs are accepted and originals retained; XML/summary do not guarantee full decoding, build files are temporary/untracked, and sensitive machine-wide payloads must stay local.
